Section 02

Department at a Glance

Establishment, programmes and academic scope — presented from the Department's Publication Edition 2022–2026.

The Department of Biomedical Sciences functions within the School of Health Sciences at The Apollo University, Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh. It brings together biotechnology, genetics, molecular biology and health informatics under a single academic roof, with the aim of training students who can work across laboratory science, clinical data and emerging health technologies.

The Department is led by Prof. R. Satyanarayana, Professor and Head. The faculty team spans cancer biology, structural bioinformatics, nanomaterials, public health and computational genomics.

Section 03

Vision, Mission & Objectives

Strategic direction for biomedical education, research, collaboration, ethics and measurable health impact.

Vision

Translating discovery into better human health.

To be recognised as a centre of excellence in biomedical education and research that produces graduates capable of translating laboratory and computational discovery into measurable improvements in human health.

Mission

  • Deliver UG and PG curricula in biotechnology, genetics and molecular biology, and health informatics with rigorous fundamentals and hands-on research.
  • Build faculty-led research across cancer biology, genomics, nanomaterials, public health and artificial intelligence in healthcare.
  • Extend training through internships, joint certifications and international placements.
  • Embed ethics, data-privacy awareness and professional standards from the first year.
  • Contribute to health and scientific literacy through outreach and public engagement.

Objectives

  • Sustain a research-active faculty with peer-reviewed publications.
  • Grow institutional MoUs for internships and joint research.
  • Strengthen the Honours by Research pathway.
  • Increase progression into higher education, research and health-technology careers.
  • Translate departmental research into demonstrable local impact.
  • Maintain quality-management practices at or above the ISO 21001:2018 benchmark cited in the digital book.
Core values

What guides the Department

Scientific rigour

Evidence-based teaching and research held to peer-reviewed standards.

Integrity & ethics

Honest conduct involving participants, data and biological material.

Collaboration

Openness to partnership across disciplines and national boundaries.

Innovation

Active pursuit of AI, nanomaterials and new tools for health outcomes.

Social responsibility

Commitment to community health literacy and scientific outreach.
